Practical Notes / For The Home Oven

How to bake bakery-quality loaves at home.

You don't need a four-ton steam injection hearth oven to get blistered ears and a singing crust. Four non-negotiable rituals from our morning bakers.

Rule 01 / Steam Control

The Heavy Dutch Oven Steam Trap

Preheat a cast-iron Dutch oven to 250°C for 45 minutes. The sealed pot traps dough moisture for the first 20 minutes, allowing maximum oven spring before you remove the lid.

Rule 02 / Dough Awareness

Read the Dough, Not the Clock

Timers lie, dough does not. Fermentation rates swing wildly with kitchen temperature. Look for rounded domed edges, aerated bubble windows, and a gentle bounce back.

Rule 03 / Scoring Technique

The 45° Curved Razor Angle

Cut with confidence using a curved razor lame tilted at 45 degrees rather than straight down. This creates the coveted crispy overhang flap ("the baker's ear").

Rule 04 / Texture Setting

The 2-Hour Patience Rule

Cutting into piping hot bread releases trapped steam, leaving the interior gummy and wet. Allow internal starches to set into silky custard at room temperature first.
