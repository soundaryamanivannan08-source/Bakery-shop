THE PURITY PLEDGE

Only four elements. Zero shortcuts.

We believe real bread only needs four pure ingredients. No dough improvers, no preservatives, and no commercial yeast shortcuts.

01

Stone-Ground Grains

100% certified organic heirloom wheat and rye sourced from New York state farms, stone-milled slow and cold to preserve live grain enzymes.

02

36-Hour Wild Levain

Fed twice daily with stone-milled rye flour. Wild lactobacillus bacteria slowly digest sugars, creating complex tart flavors and light digestibility.

03

Pure Spring Water & Salt

Mineral-rich natural spring water paired with hand-harvested Brittany grey sea salt, preserving essential micronutrients in every slice.

04

Stone Deck & Steam

Baked on blazing 480°F stone deck hearths with calibrated burst steam that locks in moisture and blister-caramelizes the outer crust.

BAKER'S CARE MASTERCLASS

How to keep your bake at its peak.

Simple techniques from our head baker to ensure that your crust stays shatteringly crisp and your crumb stays moist for days.

Oven Reheat: 350°F (175°C)

Reviving Day-Two Croissants

Never microwave a croissant. Instead, pop it onto a bare oven rack for 3 to 4 minutes. The cultured butter re-melts into the 81 laminated sheets, returning the outer crunch to fresh-from-the-hearth glory.

Store: Room Temp • Linen Wrap

Preserving Sourdough Crumb

Store whole loaves cut-side down on a wooden cutting board or wrapped in breathable linen. Never keep artisan bread in sealed plastic or inside the refrigerator — cold accelerates starch crystallization.

Freeze: Sliced • 0°F (-18°C)

Freezing for the Toaster

Slice your fresh sourdough on day one, slip parchment paper squares between slices, and seal in an airtight container. Toast directly from frozen for 2 minutes for an instant hot, steaming slice.
